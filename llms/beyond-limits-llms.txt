# Beyond Limits (BeyondAI)

> Beyond Limits, trading as BeyondAI, is an enterprise and industrial artificial
> intelligence company founded in 2014 in Glendale, California, built on cognitive
> and neuro-symbolic AI technology licensed from Caltech and NASA's Jet Propulsion
> Laboratory. Its Hybrid AI Platform combines machine learning, generative AI and
> rule-based symbolic reasoning so industrial operators receive explainable,
> auditable recommendations rather than black-box predictions. It sells into energy
> and LNG, oil and gas, refining, petrochemicals, utilities, mining, manufacturing,
> aerospace and critical infrastructure.

Important: Beyond Limits publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal,
no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/MCP contract, no self-service sign-up
and no public sandbox. The Hybrid AI Platform's low-code API, Data, ML Ops (PRE) and
KB Ops services are available only to customers under a commercial development
license, obtained through sales. Any agent attempting programmatic integration must
go through https://www.beyond.ai/contact/sales-contact.

## Products

- [Hybrid AI Platform](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/hybrid-ai): Enterprise
  platform combining generative AI, neuro-symbolic reasoning and expert knowledge
  capture; development license includes a low-code API, Data services, ML Ops (PRE)
  and KB Ops, plus a development sandbox instance licensed per domain.
- [Operations Advisor](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/operations-advisor):
  Decision support for industrial operations teams.
- [Beyond Search / Private GenAI](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/gen-ai):
  Secure enterprise knowledge intelligence and digital assistants.
- [AI in a Box](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/ai-in-a-box): On-premises
  enterprise AI infrastructure appliance.
- [DocLink](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/doclink): Document, data and process
  automation with ERP integration, from the Altec subsidiary.
- [PRE](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/pre): No-code Hybrid AI service for
  surfacing operational anomalies, predicting events and recommending actions.
- [KB Ops](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/kbops): No-code platform for subject
  matter experts to capture and operationalize domain knowledge.
- [AI Services](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/ai-services) and
  [AI Academy](https://www.beyond.ai/enterprise-ai/ai-academy).
