# Beyond Limits

Beyond Limits (trading as BeyondAI) is an enterprise and industrial artificial intelligence company founded in 2014 and headquartered in Glendale, California, built on cognitive and neuro-symbolic AI technology licensed from Caltech and NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. Its Hybrid AI Platform combines machine learning, generative AI and rule-based symbolic reasoning so industrial operators get explainable, auditable recommendations rather than black-box predictions. Products include the Hybrid AI Platform, Operations Advisor, Beyond Search generative AI, AI in a Box on-premises infrastructure, and DocLink document process automation from its Altec subsidiary. It sells into energy and LNG, oil and gas, refining, petrochemicals, utilities, mining, manufacturing, aerospace and critical infrastructure.

## API surface

Beyond Limits publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/MCP contract, self-service sign-up, sandbox or status page. The Hybrid AI Platform's low-code API, Data, ML Ops (PRE) and KB Ops services are licensed to customers under a commercial development license and obtained through sales.
